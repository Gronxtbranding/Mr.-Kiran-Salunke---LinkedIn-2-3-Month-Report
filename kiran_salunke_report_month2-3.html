Strategic outcomes
The five developments that defined this cycle
  • Visibility & content performance. 2,573 impressions and 112 likes across the seven posts (5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12) with metrics available, holding a 4.74% combined engagement rate. Post 5 led on engagement rate (7.27%) while Post 7 delivered the widest reach (578 impressions). Post 10 drew named engagement (9 likes, 4 comments) despite no impression count being supplied.
  • Network penetration. 635 senior professionals were targeted across 21 named companies spanning enterprise technology, semiconductors, industrial automation and FMCG, yielding 217 confirmed accepted connections — a 34.17% match rate against the full target list.
  • Seniority quality. Of the 217 confirmed matches, 187 (86.2%) sit at Director/Head level or above — 111 Directors/Heads, 28 Vice Presidents, 26 Presidents/MDs/Partners and 22 C-Suite/CXOs — an exceptionally senior confirmed network.
  • Data completeness. 615 of the 635 target-list professionals (96.9%) have a usable LinkedIn profile URL captured in the source data, giving strong traceability for the next stage of relationship-building.
  • Market mapping. Conversion was strongest at Emerson India (58.3%), Ather Energy (55.0%) and Whatfix/Britannia/Applied Materials (50.0% each), while high-volume targets like Qualcomm India (65 targeted, 10 confirmed) and NVIDIA India (27 targeted, 2 confirmed) remain priorities for continued follow-up.
